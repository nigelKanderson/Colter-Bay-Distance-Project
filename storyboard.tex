\section{Overview}
% ============================================================

Study question: does light colour (red vs. white) affect bat acoustic
activity, and does the answer depend on intensity or distance from the
source?

Design: 14 acoustic monitoring stations along a distance gradient
($<$0.52 km to $>$2.1 km from source), red vs. white light crossed
at five intensities (10, 30, 50, 70, 100\%). Survey season: 2022.
Eight focal species: Epfu, Laci, Lano, Myev, Mylu, Myvo, Myyu, Myci.

\section{Summary}
% ============================================================

1. Colour x intensity interaction: red light at 30-50\% produced 1.6-2.1x
   more detections than white. At 70\% and 100\%, colours converge.
   White light is flat at all intensities.

2. Distance: sites within 0.5 km had 3x fewer detections. Recovery
   appears complete by 0.5-1.0 km.

3. Community composition is driven by distance, habitat, and sky
   darkness — not by light colour.

4. Management implication: red light at 30-50\% intensity, sited >0.5 km
   from sensitive habitat, maximises bat detections. White light
   suppresses activity at any intensity.
